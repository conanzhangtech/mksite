Quill does not have accounts, replies, likes, notifications, avatars or an editor for comments. People have asked for each of these, some of them politely and twice.

I turned them down because every one of them means storing something about a reader, and I did not want to store anything.

## The one I miss

Replies. A comment thread where I cannot answer a question feels rude. For now I answer in the next post, which is clumsy.

## The one I would never add

Notifications by email. Someone would need to give me their address, and I would then be a person who has it.
