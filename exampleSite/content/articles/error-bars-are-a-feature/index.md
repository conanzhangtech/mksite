The first version of Tidemark showed one number, the stream level in centimetres, in large type. People kept ringing to ask if it was right.

I took the question as a sign the number was unclear, so I made it bigger. This did not help.

## The second version

It showed a range, drawn as a pale band behind the line. The calls stopped within a fortnight. When I asked the school secretary why, she said the band looked like it knew what it didn't know.

## A few rules I follow now

- Draw the range in a lighter shade behind the main line.
- When a sensor is unsure, say so in words next to it.
- Never hide a gap in the data. A gap is something the reader wants to know about.

Over eight weeks the questions about whether the figure was right went from about twenty a week to two.
