Before the study I had never used a screen reader for more than ten minutes. So I turned my laptop screen off for a working week and used VoiceOver.

On the first day it took me 25 minutes to find the send button in my own email. By Friday I was down to about two, and I had stopped moving my head towards the speaker.

## What I noticed

Long pages are tiring. I stopped reading to the end of anything. I jumped through headings, found the one that sounded promising and started there, just as the people in our study had done.

Pages that put the same heading, "Details", over and over were nearly unusable. I heard "Details" nine times in a row on one settings page and had no way to tell the nine apart.

## One small thing

Alt text that says "image" is worse than none. I would rather be told nothing than be told there is something I cannot have.
