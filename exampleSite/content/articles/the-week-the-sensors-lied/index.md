In the third week of July 2024 the temperature in Leeds passed 33 degrees and all six of our sensors started reporting a stream that was rising.

It was not. I walked down on the Thursday evening and the water was lower than I had ever seen it. The sensors were measuring the distance to the water with sound, and sound travels faster in hot air.

## What we did

The dull answer is that we added a temperature reading to each sensor and corrected for it. That took a weekend and a soldering iron.

The more useful answer is that we wrote the week up and put it on the dashboard. A note at the top said which readings had been wrong and for how long. Three people emailed to say it was the first time a council-type website had admitted a mistake.

## What I would do differently

I would have fitted the temperature probes at the start. They cost 60 pence each.
