Two years ago I put a paper on my site as a bare PDF link and wondered why nobody found it. Searching for its exact title turned up a Russian mirror site and nothing of mine.

Give each paper a page. Put the abstract in plain text on it, with the authors and the year, and link to the file.

## Google Scholar

Scholar reads a short list of tags in the page's head: title, authors, date, venue and the address of the PDF. Once I added them my paper showed up in about three weeks. The theme writes them from the front matter now, so you fill each field once.

## A citation people will use

Put a BibTeX block on the page. People paste it into their own papers without checking, so it had better be right.

## Keep the PDF

Don't hide it behind a viewer. A reader is fine, but leave a download link next to it. Some people want the file on their own machine, and they are usually the ones who will cite you.
