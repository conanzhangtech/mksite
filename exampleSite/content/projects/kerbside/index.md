The council published bin days as a PDF and road works as a spreadsheet, so I put both on one map. Type a postcode and Kerbside shows the next three collections and anything dug up on your street.

It was a weekend project that got fifty visits in the first week, mostly from me. Then a local newsletter linked to it and the count went to four thousand. The council later asked for the data behind it, which was a bit awkward, since the data was theirs.

The missed-collection analysis in [Where the Bins Go](/research/where-the-bins-go/) started from the complaints people sent through the site.
