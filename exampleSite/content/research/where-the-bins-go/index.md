We expected the missed collections to be spread across the area. They were not. Eleven streets made up almost half of them, and nine of the eleven had a narrow section where the lorry could not turn.

Daniel Frost, who drove a collection route for six years before joining the project, told us this in the first meeting. It took us four months of analysis to confirm it.
