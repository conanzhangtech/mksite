abstract: "We watched 24 screen reader users read long documents. Most went to the headings first and chose where to read from them. Unclear headings cost them several minutes. We suggest three changes to how authors write headings."

Twenty-four people came to the lab, and we paid for their travel. Two sessions ran over because participants wanted to keep reading the documents we had given them, which we took as a good sign about the documents and a bad sign about our schedule.
