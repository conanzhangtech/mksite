Icons used by the mksite theme.

- Brand icons (github, linkedin, instagram and others) come from Simple Icons 13.21.0 and are CC0. The marks themselves belong to their owners. Use them only to link to those services.
- Interface icons (mail, download, search and others) come from Lucide 0.468.0 and are under the ISC licence.

The licences are in this folder. To add an icon, drop an SVG here with a 24 by 24 viewBox, using `fill="currentColor"` for solid icons or `stroke="currentColor"` for line icons.
